# Crackle · Ice crackle

An ice-crackle fill, taken from the irregular lines of lattice windows and of crazed glaze on porcelain.

It is a `<div class="hao-crackle" role="presentation">`, 40px tall; the pattern tiles across and down the block it sits in.

- Its color is `pattern`, low in contrast, a fill only; no text or controls on top of it.
- Use it where a “window” is wanted: the illustration spot of an empty state, a corner of a cover, one side of a card. Choose between this and the wave pattern; different screens may take turns.
- Its lines are straight and of unequal length, the opposite of the wave’s arcs: water is soft, ice is hard. Use it when the content is quiet and cool.
- It counts as one scene—at most one per screen; see the three rules in the guide.

**Accessibility**

- It is decoration: as in the preview, give it `role="presentation"`, and a screen reader says nothing.
