A wave-pattern fill, taken from the layered water waves of the “seas and cliffs (海水江崖)” pattern at the hem of ceremonial robes.

On its own, it is a `<div class="hao-wave" role="presentation">`, 40px tall (two rows of waves); more often you add `hao-card--wave` to a card, and the waves spread along its bottom edge.

- Its color is `pattern`, low in contrast, a fill only; no text or controls on top of it.
- Use it on the screen’s most important card, in the illustration spot of an empty state, or at the bottom of a launch screen.
- Keep the height a multiple of 20px, three rows (60px) at most. Any taller, and it takes over.
- It counts as one scene—at most one per screen; see the three rules in the guide.
