The English edition of the sample screen, same structure as the Chinese one, for comparing the English typesetting rules—not a reusable component.

It demonstrates: with `lang="en"` on the page’s root element, the title automatically switches to the size and letter spacing of `title-1-en`; the seal stays a Chinese-character seal, kept as a graphic and not swapped for letters; the signature becomes a horizontal italic `colophon`; labels and buttons use sentence case, never all caps.
