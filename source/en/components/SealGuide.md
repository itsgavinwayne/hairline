Seal-face diagrams for preparing seal artwork: one each for intaglio, relief, and the four-character seal, with sizes and character areas marked. They are drawings for the person making the seal, not components for the interface.

- The canvas is 48×48, the seal face takes the middle 40×40, and the four corners are the same small, unequal corners as the system’s seals.
- The dashed frame is the character area (字区): the characters must fall inside it. The intaglio area is larger (34×34), and the characters fill it; the relief area is a step smaller (30×29.5), and the characters stay clear of the border.
- The relief bottom edge is heavier than the other three, so the bottom of the character area rises half a unit to match.
- This system **does not supply seal script**. Which characters go in the seal face, and how they are written, is prepared by the person making the seal; the rules are in the “Seal design” section of the guide.
- Blank seal-face files are in the Seals group under resources; you can set characters directly on them.

**Accessibility**

- As in the preview, give each diagram `role="img"` and an `aria-label` on the `<svg class="hao-sealguide">`; a screen reader treats it as one named image.
- `role="img"` folds the character areas and the size labels inside the diagram into that one image instead of reading them one by one; the explanation of the sizes goes in the paragraph beside the diagram, as in the preview.
