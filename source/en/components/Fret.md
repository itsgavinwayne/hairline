A band of the fret, its pattern born on the bronzes of the Shang and Zhou dynasties (商周) and later woven into the borders of brocade.

Use `<div class="hao-fret" role="presentation">`, in gold ink. The height is fixed at 12px and the width follows the container; keep the width a multiple of 20px, so the pattern is never cut short.

- Set it at the head of a page or a long piece, just under the large title; or in the footer of an About page or a splash screen.
- Never bend it into a ring, run it upright, or place it in a button, an input field, or a list row.
- It counts as one scene—at most one per screen; see the three rules in the guide.
