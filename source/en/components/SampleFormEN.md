The English edition of the form page, same structure as the Chinese one, for comparing how form copy is written in English—not a reusable component.

It demonstrates: the steps are numbered 1, 2, 3; labels run no longer than two words and buttons no longer than three, all in sentence case; helper text states the consequence and the fix (Don’t add the extension), with no apology. Task screens carry no scene, in English as in Chinese.
