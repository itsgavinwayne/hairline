The mark for Hairline itself: a seal with a moon and a waterline on its face. The three files use different colors: `hairline-mark.svg` is cinnabar (#b83224) for light backgrounds; `hairline-mark-night.svg` is the night theme cinnabar (#ff8a75) for dark backgrounds; `hairline-mark-ink.svg` is monochrome blue-black (#161b24) for single-color printing. Use it only to refer to this system itself; a product made with this system uses its own seal as its mark. See the `Logo` component for usage.
