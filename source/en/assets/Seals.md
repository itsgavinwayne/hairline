Two blank seal faces to use as bases for seal artwork: `seal-blank-baiwen.svg` is the base for an intaglio seal (白文): a solid cinnabar seal face with the characters cut out; `seal-blank-zhuwen.svg` is the base for a relief seal (朱文): only the border, with the bottom edge slightly heavier, and the characters drawn inside in cinnabar. The canvas is 48×48, the seal face occupies the central 40×40, and the cinnabar is #b83224. See the “Seal design” section of the Guide and the `SealGuide` component for dimensions, character area, and method.
