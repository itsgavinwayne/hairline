中文

白文：整块朱砂色，字镂空。字区四周留红 3 个单位，笔画粗，字撑满。

朱文：只有边栏，字用朱砂色画。边栏宽 1.8，底边 3；字离边栏 3 个单位，笔画细，和边栏差不多粗。

四字：两行两列，自右上起竖读。字与字之间留 1.6 个单位，和笔画间的空隙差不多。

English

Intaglio: a solid cinnabar face with the characters cut out. Leave 3 units of red all round; strokes are thick and fill the area.

Relief: a frame only, with the characters drawn in cinnabar. The frame is 1.8 wide and 3 at the bottom; keep the characters 3 units inside it, with thin strokes about as heavy as the frame.

Four characters: two columns, read top to bottom starting at the upper right. Leave 1.6 units between characters, about the same as the gaps between strokes.
