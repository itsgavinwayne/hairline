中文

札记会同步到哪些设备

登录同一个账号的所有设备。同步在连上网络后自动进行，不需要手动操作。

删除的札记能找回吗

删除后会在「最近删除」里保留三十天。

怎么导出全部内容

在设置里选择「导出」，可以得到一个包含全部札记的压缩包。

English

Which devices do notes sync to

Every device signed in to the same account. Syncing happens on its own once you’re online.

Can I get a deleted note back

Deleted notes stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

How do I export everything

Choose Export in Settings to download an archive of all your notes.
