中文

中间的记号叫「鱼尾」，是雕版书页版心上用来对折的标记。

另一种是文武线：一粗一细，取自宋版书的版框，用在一章、一组内容的开头。

English

The mark in the middle is a “fishtail”, the fold guide printed at the center of a woodblock page.

The other kind is the double rule, one thick line and one thin, from the page frame of Song printed books. Use it at the head of a chapter or a group.
