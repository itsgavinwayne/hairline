A morning in fog

Four days before Minor Snow (小雪), the Bingwu year

I opened the window this morning and the building across the way was only a faint outline. The fog had taken everything far off, leaving just this small patch in front of me: the windowsill, a pot of asparagus fern, a glass of water just poured.

Most days I feel that seeing far is what it means to see clearly. Today I couldn’t see far, and so I looked closely at what was near.

Though made by human hands, it seems to come from heaven.

Ji Cheng, The Craft of Gardens

Landscape painters say the most important part of the paper is the place left unpainted. Fog is probably the blank the world leaves for itself.

By nine the fog had lifted and the building came back. I finished the water in my glass and started the first thing of the day.
