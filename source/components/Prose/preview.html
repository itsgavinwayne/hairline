雾里的早晨

丙午年 小雪前四日

早上推开窗，对面的楼只剩一个淡淡的轮廓。雾把远处的东西都收走了，只留下眼前这一小块：窗台、一盆文竹、一杯刚倒的水。

平时总觉得要看得远才算看清楚。今天看不远，反倒把近处的东西看了个仔细。文竹新抽了一枝，细得像一根线，昨天还没有。

留白

画山水的人说，纸上最要紧的是没画的地方。雾大概就是天地自己留的白。它什么也不添，只是把多余的先拿开。

等到九点钟，雾散了，楼又回来了，一扇扇窗户都清清楚楚。我把杯子里的水喝完，开始做今天的第一件事。

丙午冬印

English

A morning in fog

Four days before Minor Snow, the Bingwu year

I opened the window this morning and the building across the street was only a faint outline. The fog had taken away everything far off and left one small patch in front of me: the windowsill, a pot of asparagus fern, a glass of water just poured.

I usually think that seeing clearly means seeing far. Today I couldn’t see far, so I looked closely at what was near. The fern had put out a new shoot, thin as a thread, that wasn’t there yesterday.

Blank space

Landscape painters say the most important part of the paper is the part left unpainted. Fog may be the blank space the world leaves for itself. It adds nothing. It only sets aside what isn’t needed yet.

By nine the fog had lifted and the building was back, every window sharp. I finished the water and started on the first thing of the day.
